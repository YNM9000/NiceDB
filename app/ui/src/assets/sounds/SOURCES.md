# Sounds

`marimba/` and `ciotola/` (hand chimes): `done`, `applied`, `error`, `warn`, `notify`, `unlock`, `lock`, `copy`, `toggle`, `drop`, `jingle`. WAV, 16 bit, 48 kHz, mono.

Every sound is cut from real recordings released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): no synthesis, no generated audio. Credit is not required; it is given here anyway.

- Marimba and hand chimes: Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL) by Versilian Studios LLC, CC0 1.0, https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL. Marimba (Outrigger mallets, medium): F3, C4, G4, B4. Hand Chimes (sustained): F#3, A#3, D4, E4, F#4, G#4, C5.
- Foley, Freesound, CC0 1.0:
  - "briefcase locks.wav" by xyzr_kx, https://freesound.org/people/xyzr_kx/sounds/156649/ (the latch in warn, unlock, lock)
  - "Light Switch ON / OFF" by FillSoko, https://freesound.org/people/FillSoko/sounds/257958/ (toggle)
  - "Wooden Tap" by AidanSounds, https://freesound.org/people/AidanSounds/sounds/704935/ (copy)
  - "Wood Pat 07" by chaosportal, https://freesound.org/people/chaosportal/sounds/137084/ (applied)
  - "Putting Down Book" by Sheyvan, https://freesound.org/people/Sheyvan/sounds/480891/ (drop)

The recordings were trimmed, filtered, tuned by resampling to D major pentatonic, layered and levelled.
